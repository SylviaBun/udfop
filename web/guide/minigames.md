This page lists out minigames included in Daggerfall Online as well as the mechanics associated with them. Any information which may be inaccurate is as a result of player-driven submissions, so if something here is wrong please open an [issue](https://github.com/tau-samsara/udfop/issues/new?template=content.yml&title=%5BContent%5D%20&page=https%3A%2F%2Ftau-samsara.github.io%2Fudfop%2F%23%2Fabout)

## Iliac Hand Rules:

**1**  The game always lasts 6 turns, and at the end of each turn played cards are revealed simultaneously. Your magicka allowance per turn is equal to the turn count, so at turn 1 you get 1 magicka, turn 2 you get 2, and so on.

**2** There are three card zones, each split into two sides - you and the opponent. The zones have their own passive effects, determined randomly when the game starts, and visible at all times.

**3** There are two kinds of cards: Units, which have a power, cost, and effect each, and Spells, which just have a Cost and Effect, and are removed from the game after their effect resolves.

**4** At the end of the game, the player with the highest total Power in a zone wins the zone, and whoever wins the most zones at this time wins the game.
