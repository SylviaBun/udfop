Banners ||| Banners are capture points, arena companies, and HUD item notifications. A banner point is raised by standing with no living enemy within 8 m for 20 seconds, a contested banner freezes, and a half-raised banner falls back if left. The arena's Red Banner and Blue Banner companies take fighters at the gate for free, field 24 fighters each who climb the season ladder, score points for ladder wins (3 for a Tier Champion, 10 for the Grand Champion), show pennants on the versus bar, and hang their colours around the ring and crowd; the banner with more points at season's end wins and its fighters wear the laurel next year, while fighters can quit anytime but cannot join the other banner until next season, and exhibitions pit Red against Blue. Additionally, when a Rare, Legendary, Aetheric, Artifact, or Gilded item reaches your pack, wagon, or Materials Bag from any source (except moving items between your own storage, pack, wagon, or bag), a loot banner slides in at the right edge showing its picture, tier colour, pips, name, item type, and "New to your codex" for first finds (suppressing the crosshair pickup feed); Aetheric, Artifact, and Gilded items trigger a larger glowing banner and fanfare, up to three show at once ordered by tier, and Loot banners can be moved or hidden via the HUD editor (Alt+U).
Characters ||| Online characters keep their own Renown, professions, and Stores, and an account can hold more than one; deleting an online character deletes its Renown, professions, and Stores, and a character with open market business cannot be deleted until it is settled. Card tables no longer lock players out: the red table plays for gold and can only be staked at by realm characters, while realm characters can also play for chips at the green table alongside everyone else.
Front Door ||| There is one front door under both skins, and you can now decorate the ground right outside your own front door.
Houses ||| Doors say Residence and list Go in and Buy it when pointed at, a quest's house reads "The <name> Residence" and is never for sale, a customs-brought house cannot be sold for nothing, pressing Sell twice does not error, shelves in ordinary houses do not swallow clicks, entering houses no longer drops players into the void or dark, and a Knightly Order's house is not offered for purchase at its owner's door. When a family member or online character dies permanently, their house and contents (door, bed, chests, furniture, and cupboards) pass to their heir; if the heir already owns a house in that region, the inherited house waits until they sell theirs.
Pause Menu ||| The enhanced pause menu displays guild reputation and features an online mailbox represented by an envelope beside the hourglass. When developers send a message, the envelope displays a count and a line in world chat announces post arrival; messages can be opened to read or accessed from anywhere by typing /mail in chat on either menu style, and messages with unclaimed items cannot be accidentally discarded.
Performance ||| A GPU memory leak from opening the map in a dungeon or building was fixed, long sessions and long journeys no longer accumulate video or GPU memory because left areas are released over time, settings are read faster everywhere, and people, guards, and foes do not pop out of view at screen edges. Opening the Overworld no longer freezes the game as nearby dungeon models load one per frame after the view rises, deep snow processing is disabled while the Overworld is open, and saving in snowy areas is faster because snow-track data is recompressed only when changed.
Reach ||| The reach carries the crouch dip, and deserts no longer get snow drifting in from nearby snowy regions, keeping the edge of a desert adjacent to a snowy area bare.
Ships ||| The bank's ship loan has been removed, and a crash when another player's ship list changed while standing on their ship was fixed. The Cast the net prompt does not appear at the helm, while aiming guns, or boarding; ship audio does not cut out when getting under way or slowing; nearby boats are not left silent; leaving the cabin no longer strands players in a void; standing in water or on a quay beside a moored galleon does not count as aboard; and small details on far-off ships are not drawn below a pixel. The carrack features ten shuttered gunports with platform guns, a wheel and rudder that turn together, and a rig with spritsail, fore/main courses, main topsail, and lateen mizzen. The large boat features seven sail plans on a new hull, tiller steering with a step for the helmsman, and seven swivel guns on its gunwale (three per side, one at the bow).
Snow ||| With Snowless Swamps and Jungles switched on, swamps and jungles no longer get deep snow on the ground or falling snow in winter.
