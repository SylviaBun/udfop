Features ||| Features is the screen where every enhanceable feature is switched. It is a panel of tiles with on and off shown as green and red, an All off button and a search. Descriptions are in plain words, and Daggerfall Unity's own options end with their default, such as "Off by default in Daggerfall Unity." Under Combat, a Steel Helm switch picks Closed (a visor and a blue plume, the default) or Open (a nasal helm with a mail aventail that shows the face).
Morrowind Body ||| The Morrowind body is posed for climbing: hanging shows both hands gripping the ledge with palms down and feet against the wall, shimmying is hand over hand, climbing reaches hands up in turn with the opposite foot following, and moves such as a leap reach, a ledge pull-over and a vault tuck are posed. A failing grip makes the hands tremble and feet scrabble, a hard catch swings the body, in first person the hands reach the ledge seen, and other players see the same. Turning to a crowd no longer stalls a frame. Steel armour now shows as a full suit of plate (breastplate, pauldrons, gauntlets, greaves, boots and helm) made for the port; Silver and Elven armour keep the Morrowind steel they wore before.
