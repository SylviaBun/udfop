Standing ||| Standing is how regions and the law see the player. A negative standing now recovers faster, a point every 7 days instead of every 112, with the law or a guild. A screen shows every region that knows the player's name, how it sees them and what it will cost, every change from a crime, sentence, penance, contract or raid defence is announced in a line, and the Enhanced Plus Standing page has a Dress line showing what the outfit is doing. For a seat, a low Tithe (half the cap or less) gives +2 each week, a high one (over three quarters) gives minus 3, and a seat held without a challenge gives +5, with the seat's board saying which way the Standing moved since the last Turning.
Standing Cards ||| Standing cards are no longer shown for ground objects.
Static Models ||| Static models are batched by texture into one mesh per pixel.
Stats Card ||| The enhanced paperdoll card has a Stats button that flips it to a stats page. The back shows damage, hit chance, critical chance and what a critical does, backstab chance and expected damage, with a table per swing direction (sideways, thrust, down-right, down-left, overhead) and a bow's release, and a breakdown of weapon roll, material, Strength, proficiency, race, attack term, foe armour and dodging. Defence shows overall armour, the seven part numbers, dodging, biography and health with attributes and skills, and hit and damage are scored against a foe of the player's level in four armour classes using real armour numbers. It is Enhanced skin only.
Status Box ||| The status box crash and an empty status box were fixed.
Status Readout ||| The status readout is live.
Steady Shadows ||| Steady Shadows is a switch in the Enhanced lighting tile, on by default, that stops shadows popping on and off by redrawing every shadow every frame. The nearest lamps keeping full shadows went from 8 to 12, lamps at almost the same distance no longer trade shadows, the player's own shadow no longer jumps from lamp to lamp, and eyes adjust to light changes more slowly. It costs more graphics work and about 48 MB of memory, so it can be turned off if frame rate drops, and the player's silhouette now falls only from the two nearest lamps.
Steel Brigandine ||| With Roleplay and Realism: Items on, a steel Brigandine Jerkin draws as a red riveted knee-length brigandine, the second model made for this port alone after the Dwarven Thunderlock. It shows in third person, on other players and on the paper doll, the body moves with the chest and the skirt with the hips so the hem swings, and it is fitted to the player's own torso when put on. A mesh or texture of the same name in the Data Files still replaces it, and on the ground and in pack icons the jerkin still shows the steel cuirass.
Stonebreaker ||| Stonebreaker is a rank 100 reward that makes quarrying always give Cut Stone.
Stores ||| The Stores hold what the player gathers, up to 5,000 of each material per character, and are kept in town, so the Stores page in any town moves materials. The page has filters, search and sorting, and Withdraw to pack takes up to 200 at a time, every material able to be withdrawn, with herbs coming out as Daggerfall's own northern or southern ingredient that works in the potion maker. Emptying the bag into the pack is possible from the Stores page anywhere, and a withdrawal whose answer was lost is sent again when opening the page, never minted twice.
Stream Build ||| The stream build breathes, and a warm pixel build yields to the frame.
Street Shield ||| The street's shield was fixed.
Strike Spells ||| Online, a weapon's strike spell damages a monster another player spawned, with Cast When Strikes weapons reaching the whole spell, though other effects do not yet. Strike enchantments do not fire in duels.
Sun Baby ||| Sun Baby is a developer-staged online event (/event sunbaby, ended with /event sunbaby off). The sky turns nursery blue with drifting flowers and a giggling baby-faced sun rises in the east while the weather clears and the land is lit like a sunny day at any hour. The event cycles: the sun turns evil and rains harmless fireballs on Daggerfall, calms, then becomes Todd Howard, with everyone online seeing the same phase, and the sun snaps between its faces in a second.
Supplies ||| Offline, General Stores and Alchemists sell supplies with Climates and Calories, and online they arrive in a later update.
Survival Thirst ||| SURV-THIRST1 fixed a thirst fault from a field report and its audit.
Swimmer ||| The swimmer was fixed in 0.1.4582.
Swimming ||| With no fatigue left in water the player now loses a tenth of their health every few seconds and not instantly, and sinking into water no longer puts the view under the ground.
Swing ||| Weapon swing is click-or-hold by default, with a sane swing law (SWING-LAW) and a fix to the desktop swing.
Tab Key ||| Mac's four reports about the Tab key were fixed.
Table ||| A table is now a wall.
Tactics ||| With Enhanced AI at most two melee and two ranged foes attack the player at once, and the rest ring them and wait. Melee foes step in when the player is open and back out after their blow, with a patience clock limiting waits, a hurt foe gives ground and circles before coming back, and animals and cowardly people flee at low health while guards, undead, daedra and constructs never do. Archers and casters back away from a closing melee fighter, fight when cornered and seek a clear shot. With Enhanced AI off, foes behave as in classic Daggerfall.
