Alchemy Station ||| An alchemy station brews Daggerfall's twenty potions, at any Alchemist's for 50 gold a brew or at a home station. Ingredients come from the player's Stores. A brew makes one potion, two from Journeyman and three as a Master, and may come out Potent with +25% magnitude for healing, stamina, strength or power potions. Daggerfall's own potion maker is unchanged and earns nothing toward Alchemy. The stations name the craft they serve, "Provisioning" at the fire and the alchemy station.
Arcane Essence ||| Arcane Essence is a crafting material. It stays in the player's Stores, never the pack, and sells on the market like any Stores material. From Enchanting 50, an enchanting station spends 2 Arcane Essence to roll one line of a known Magic piece again, or 5 for a Rare's, which is the Mages Guild's Reforge paid in Essence.
Area Spells ||| Area spells hit everything inside an area. Buff-only Area spells also land on the caster. Area at Range and Around Caster now show what they catch, with people up to 4.45 m and the boss at 5.8 m. The shockwave spreads across what the spell hit, bigger areas show more, and area heals fill the circle. With Morrowind data installed, spells use Morrowind's own effects: the glow around the caster and on the hands, the bolt a target spell flies as, the hit on whoever it lands on, and the burst of an area spell.
Banishment ||| Banishment is a punishment only murder and treason can bring, and it lifts after 30 days. A pardon can be bought at a temple in that region, where the watch does not enter. Online, banishment follows the realm's calendar so a wrong computer clock cannot end it early. Four long online waits were shortened, banishment among them, along with bank hours, Daedra summoning and the King of Worms' messenger.
Castle Daggerfall ||| Castle Daggerfall had a softlock that was fixed in 0.1.3751, and the two towers in its courtyard are whole again.
Characters ||| Online characters keep their own Renown, professions and Stores, and an account can hold more than one; the first online release allowed only one. Deleting an online character also deletes its Renown, professions and Stores, and a character with market business still open cannot be deleted until that is settled.
Climbing ||| Climbing lets the player pull up onto ledges, wall tops, parapets and roofs by pressing Jump at them, and hang from edges. Climbing skill sets reach, speed, grip time and how long a fall can still be caught, with every point past 100 making the player faster up to about 1.4 times at 200. From the ground a shoulder-height ledge is reachable at skill 0 and one above the head at 100, a full pack lowers reach, and a roof can be climbed from its eave up to 50 degrees. With Roleplay and Realism on, the player cannot climb while holding a weapon, and online others see the player on the wall and hear them climb. Climbing is not possible while fast travelling on the Overworld, a journey cannot be started while hanging on a wall, and climbing works as before at walking pace.
Coasts ||| Walking or sailing along a coastline no longer stalls the game while the surf is rebuilt, and the far coasts were fixed. Towns on the coast stand on their own land, not dragged down toward the sea. Coastal towns no longer stand behind cliffs at the beach line; the strand rises to them gently.
Companions ||| Companions are allies who follow the player: a boat's hands taken ashore and sworn revenants. Up to three slots are shared between sworn revenants and any ship's crew ashore. They attack anything hostile, never take damage from the player's own blows, follow through doors by the path the player walked, and arrive and leave through a swirling violet portal. A companion who falls is carried off to recover for 8 hours, and loyalty matters, since a neglected one deserts and a disloyal Unhinged, Craven or Brutal one may turn on the player. A pause-menu Stats page shows who is At your side or Away, with Send away, Call and Release, and each companion's pack has a weight limit by strength. Companions do not follow the player into the Burning Court, and a companion through the carrack's middle house leads down to her gun deck.
Crafting ||| Crafting received fixes to how crafts settle. A craft whose answer was lost settles on its own, a slow answer still pays the smith or furnisher when it lands, a station's fee the purse cannot cover is shown, and a recipe cannot be changed while the heat or plane is under way. A maker's mark is part of the piece's signed record, and a Masterwork piece, or any furniture a Master Joiner makes, carries its maker's name. Refining for a craft costs no extra station fee. When short of something, the box names the raw good needed (for example "short of Iron x2"), not the ingot, and a refused refining step is named in the message.
Dungeons ||| Dungeon holes were closed, and foes, quest foes included, no longer spawn stuck inside dungeon crates. Stepping out and back in during the same session leaves defeated enemies and emptied chests that way for up to an hour, as in other dungeons online. Towns no longer sit in pits behind sheer walls of earth.
Enhanced Combat Visuals ||| A concealed foe is drawn instead of vanishing. How concealed players are drawn has not changed.
Eye of the Beholder ||| Eye of the Beholder is a mod, version 2.1, ported partially. Fixes made the body stand properly instead of as a statue or on its head, repaired the hovering body, the chop and the turn, added the cart, and stopped the camera clipping through walls and ceilings. The third-person sprite no longer flickers between two directions when starting to run or strafe at an angle, an attack plays one swing instead of a swing and a follow-through, and the "PingPong Offset" setting no longer does anything.
Fast Travel ||| Fast travel was fixed so that arriving in a raided town no longer crashes. It shows the journey's days again, which pass on the player's clock so the player arrives in the world's present, and cautious travel heals again.
Field Bugs ||| Field bugs are batches of fixes from reports. Fixes in 0.1.4690 (field bugs 0928d) and a later batch dated 2026-10-07 covered the board crash, potion names, fishing, black screenshots, guild halls and skinning indoors.
Gathering Nodes ||| Every map square now holds twice the herb patches, trees and ore veins each day in every climate, with the daily limit per profession unchanged. Each node can still be worked once a day by each character, so the next harvest is wherever the next node stands.
Harbours ||| Port towns now have ships moored along their shore, sails stowed, the same for every player. A moored ship stays a while and sets sail, and merchantmen sail in and tie up at a free berth. Near a port merchantmen come and go, navy ships patrol the approaches and pirates lurk offshore, and a berthed navy ship sails out to meet a pirate while a merchant flees. A coastal seat can raise a Harbour, making the town a port for the holder's members. Where the sea floor shelves gently, berths and quays lie further from shore, and a port whose harbour lies far from any street no longer searches for its dock four times a second.
Hoods ||| The hood of a worn cloak or robe can be raised and lowered from its card in the pack. A raised hood shields a vampire from the sun and protects a character whose class has the Damage from Sunlight disadvantage. Bare-headed, the sun works as before, and a hood carried in the pack, not worn, doesn't count.
Inventory ||| The inventory drag carries the icon, and the hold that sometimes did not take was fixed. Stack splitting, double-click equip and item lock were added (the last two Enhanced Plus only). It works on phones, with the character picture hidden until Body is tapped and tabs scrolling in one row, and the wagon, storage and Materials Bag have a search box and category menu, with Shift-click moving a whole stack from the pack into them. Item cards compare what the item would change against what it would replace, showing up or down by how much, "new", and the lines that would be lost. Gear can be marked as junk from its card and "Sell junk" at a shop puts every junk piece on the counter in one press, and quick loot has a "Take gear" setting (All, Magic+, Rare+) for the take-all key.
Invisibility ||| Invisibility works online. Party members can interact with each other while under Chameleon, Shadow or Invisibility. Concealed players outside the party stay hidden, as before.
Lantern ||| The lantern sits at the waist. The carrack carries seven lanterns, so she is lit at night at sea.
Loot Drops ||| Loot drops are what enemies leave. In the latest changes regular enemies keep 3 in 4 of their items offline, up from half, and online 5/8 to 7/8 by Renown. They drop blue, yellow and orange items more often, stepping up at Renown 10, 20, 30 and 40 and using the rolling player's Renown (the host's in a shared dungeon), and gear an enemy was wearing can drop as Magic, Rare or better, even from champions, elites and bosses. A full body keeps a potion or rest supply ahead of plain gear. Earlier, normal enemies were capped at 3 items, gold included (5 in an Elite Dungeon), champions stopped always dropping a Rare, and ordinary foes left each item only half the time with gold untouched. Luck multiplies drop chances, half at Luck 0 and half again at Luck 100, instead of adding a flat amount.
Loot Rarity ||| Loot rarity is a Diablo-style ladder over Daggerfall's loot, built on Unleveled Loot's law and on by default, with the ladder shown on the items. Clothing can drop as Magic and Rare with lines of its own: standing with a social group, warmth and weatherproofing (for Climates & Calories), Personality, and street skills, with footwear leaning to movement skills. Superior and Masterwork clothing the player makes comes out Magic and Rare, as crafted weapons and armour already did. Six Legendary garments each have a power: the Alik'r Wayfarer's Robes (heat felt 20 less), the Wrothgar Pass Mantle (cold felt 20 less), the Vestments of Stendarr (Restoration spells cost 25% less), the Pilgrim's Sandals (tire 25% slower, falls hurt half as much), the Silks of Queen Barenziah (+5 standing with every group) and the Shade's Cowl (+25 Stealth while hooded). With the loot rarity setting off, clothing stays exactly as in Daggerfall. Resistance from rolled gear counts up to 45% per element, and only fills an Aetheric piece's resistance up to that same 45%. Hone raises one line within its own range, and some Rares and Legendaries carry an empty socket. One Rare or Legendary item in twelve from a body or a pile is cursed, with one extra bonus line and one drawback: a bad reputation, bad reactions, damage or wear in holy places, weaker blows against a kind of foe, or a health leech.
Low Poly Trees ||| Low Poly Trees is SquidKamer's mod that makes trees, bushes and stumps real 3D models, 253 of them across every climate. Each wild tree has its own size and turn, up close it is the 3D tree and further out its picture fades in, and with Seasons of the Iliac Bay it takes autumn, spring and snow. Near trees are drawn only where the player looks. It is on by default under Mods, turning it off brings back classic trees at the next world load, felling for Logging and cover work as before, and leaves and bark are painted from the player's own Daggerfall files. Yard trees use it too. The trees no longer hang over roads, tracks or town streets while standing exactly where they did, following the Clear roadsides option, which is on by default.
Magic Repair ||| Magic repairs work online. Enchanted items still mend only with Allow Magic Repairs, which is on by default and always on online. Armour, clothing and jewellery still mend Daggerfall's 1 point at a time, one piece every four game minutes and only for the player.
Main Menu ||| The main menu has Install beside Play, a profile mark that is a portrait of the last character, and Patreon and Ko-fi icons in the top-left corner that open their pages in the browser. It is no longer cut off on short windows, and the logo sits below the corner icons on narrow windows. The buttons are carved stone slabs in the Enhanced Plus style, all one width, and on phones they are a little more compact so the whole menu fits above the Screenshots and About buttons.
Marks ||| Marks are the online world's own currency, kept by the account service and belonging to the account, so all of a player's characters share them, held only on a registered account. A player earns 50 Marks for each Oblivion Gate counted on the record, up to two gates a day, and can sell them at any Bank of the Empire at 8 gold a Mark, up to 300 a day. They cannot be bought with gold. Marks held for a bid or an order always go back to the account. They were later renamed Drakes and, in 0.1.5580, silver, with every balance carried over unchanged.
Materials ||| The new ores, ingots and stone are new items drawn with Daggerfall's own pictures. Refining never turns a material into a lower tier. A temper costs half the piece's recipe's main material, rounded up (a Steel Longsword takes 2 Steel Ingots, a Leather Cuirass 3 Cured Leather), needs that recipe's rank, and pays craft XP.
Meditation Candle ||| The Meditation Candle has three uses. Lit from the pack, the player rests and kneels six seconds for half their magicka. It no longer refills what a smith won't repair.
Morrowind Body ||| The Morrowind body is posed for climbing: hanging shows both hands gripping the ledge with palms down and feet against the wall, shimmying is hand over hand, climbing reaches hands up in turn with the opposite foot following, and moves such as a leap reach, a ledge pull-over and a vault tuck are posed. A failing grip makes the hands tremble and feet scrabble, a hard catch swings the body, in first person the hands reach the ledge seen, and other players see the same. Turning to a crowd no longer stalls a frame. Steel armour now shows as a full suit of plate (breastplate, pauldrons, gauntlets, greaves, boots and helm) made for the port; Silver and Elven armour keep the Morrowind steel they wore before. Attacks keep up with the player's attack speed, and other players' fast attacks play at their pace too. Casting a spell plays the Morrowind casting animation through to the end, with the spell leaving the hands on the cast's release, and the steel plate no longer T-poses in game.
Morrowind Models ||| Morrowind models are the Morrowind rig brought into the game: its hands, weapon, movement, jump animation, arms, shield, held torch, sneak idle and head fix, with its asset layer loading by range and extracting its records once. The Morrowind lane was brought to one to one in a parity-audit fix arc, and loads got faster. Shift-clicking an item in the pack drops it in front of the player as itself, each showing its own picture, its Morrowind model when Morrowind data is attached and its classic picture otherwise. Rarity shows on the ground: Magic-or-better items get an outline in their tier's colour and Rare-or-better ones stand the same line of light as the world boss's spoils. Items come to rest against walls, stop moving while the game is paused and ride along with a body on a moving deck. Settings are under Features > Physical Items by demifiend000: turn it off, choose which kinds of loot spill from bodies, how hard it is thrown and how big items are.
Pack ||| The pack has nine pages, and a pack crash was fixed.
Pirates ||| A beaten pirate tries to run about as long as before she must strike, not 1.6 times as long. A cautious brigantine no longer attacks merchant carracks, and from level 5 raids are on galleons only. The Carrack (the pirate flagship and the merchant carrack) and the Large Boat (the pirate sloop and the coasting trader) are new models, the carrack fully textured and the boat clinker-built. The pirate sloop now closes to 50 m, where it was 55, because the new boat is a taller target.
Prices ||| Prices are said in real time, and NaN prices were fixed.
Privateer's Hold ||| Online, every character starts Privateer's Hold on their own, with its enemies alive, its doors shut and its loot untouched. Local chat in the Hold tells the player that nobody can hear them there.
Professions ||| The Professions page shows all thirteen professions. At ranks 50 and 100 the player chooses a specialisation, the first choice free and later changes costing 1,000 Marks (silver today) and taking a week, with the old one working until then. A toast says what each harvest brought, a banner marks ranks 25, 50, 75 and 100, the first harvest each session says where goods went (the Stores) and which key opens them, and the Down arrow opens Professions and Stores on either skin. Professions listed as practised grew from Herbalism, Mining and Logging to include Smithing, Carpentry, Fishing, Masonry, Cooking and Jewelcrafting, with the page explaining how to gather each. Mining, Logging, Herbalism (the Basket included), Fishing and Hunting no longer stop for the day, and the chip under the compass and the Professions tab show the day's count without a limit. Some crafts were merged so there are five instead of eight: a merged craft keeps the higher of its two XP totals, offers four choices at a rank each named for the craft it acts on, and a player may still take two crafts past Journeyman, now two of five. Each rank's choice is the higher craft's where it chose, else the other's.
Puddles ||| Puddles follow their art. Town puddles were dried and the Enhanced water was replaced with a translucent version with swell, shore foam and ripples, audited with its cost measured.
Repair Kits ||| Repair Kits are made in every metal but Warforged Steel. A kit mends the most-worn weapon or armour of its metal by a quarter of its condition, though never past about three quarters, and a Steel kit mends chain too. Using one asks which piece to mend whenever more than one could use it, with a list on the classic inventory and a Mend which? window on the enhanced one, worn pieces first then the pack. If everything it could mend is at three quarters or more, the kit is kept, and a kit used from a hotbar mends what is worn first. Kits no longer mend arrows, and one used from a corpse or chest mends the player's own gear. At the anvil, materials that can't be made yet are dimmed, Weapons, Armour, Tools and Repair Kits are 39 pieces instead of 308 rows behind a metal picker, the first-make bonus (+500 XP) is paid once per piece per tier, and a kit made there has a quality shown on its card with how far it mends. Kits already owned and field kits found in loot work as before.
Repairs ||| Every smith and guild now charges a third of Daggerfall's repair price, down from two thirds earlier, online and offline. Instant repair is on by default, so smiths hand gear back straight away. Repairs Objects mends the gear being worn, and weapons mend at a dagger's pace.
Reputation ||| Guild and faction reputation recovers over time away even when the clock catches up before faction data loads.
Restore ||| The restore's lift was fixed. The Welkynd Stone (fills magicka) and the Varla Stone (restores every enchanted item worn, magic items' charges included) can turn up in the deepest places.
Roads ||| A road crash (ROAD-CRASH) was fixed, and roads are painted onto new terrain about five times faster. Roads and tracks no longer climb every hill.
Salvage ||| A sunk ship leaves her wreckage afloat beside her casks, larger and paler than a cask. Sailing through wreckage hauls it aboard for Carpenter's Stores (2 to 6) and powder for fire barrels, letting the player repair to full and refill their fire barrels without making port. If swimming, the stores go into the pack one at a time and what is too heavy is lost, a fast travel or a door first has the crew stow wreckage from ships the player sank, and online everyone in the room sees the wreckage and whoever reaches it first hauls it in. A button on the Salvage page salvages every Magic item at once, asking first.
Ship Strength ||| Every ship takes 60% more punishment, with hulls and sails 1.6 times as strong, for the player's boats and every pirate, merchantman and navy ship. Sea fights last longer and leave more time to manoeuvre, brace and board, with the odds unchanged. A carrack's broadside still hits as hard as before, now from five guns a side instead of seven.
Ships ||| Ships had assorted fixes: the Cast the net prompt no longer appears at the helm, while aiming guns or boarding, the ship's sound no longer cuts out when getting under way or slowing, no boat is left silent until it moves, leaving the ship's cabin no longer strands the player in a black void, and standing in water or on a quay beside a moored galleon no longer counts as aboard. Small details on far-off ships are no longer drawn below a pixel. The carrack was rebuilt with ten gunports, each with its own shutter and a gun on a platform behind every port, a wheel and rudder that turn together, and a rig of a spritsail, fore and main courses, a main topsail and a lateen mizzen. The large boat was rebuilt with all seven of its sail plans on a new hull, a tiller (a raked rudder on its sternpost, with the helmsman on a step at its stern) and seven swivel guns on its gunwale, three a side and one at its bow.
Sigils ||| Sigils were added in 0.1.4193. Legendary, Aetheric, artifact and Sigil pieces can't be tempered, and a Masterwork keeps its maker's quality.
Smithing ||| Smithing is a crafting profession at an Anvil section beside the Forge on the Stores page, done in a Weaponsmith's or Armorer's (50 gold a craft) or a home with a Forge station. Weapons, plate and shields are made in every metal from Iron to Warforged Steel, chain armour is made from Steel for three quarters of plate's ingots, and tools (Wood-Axe, Pick-Axe, Sickle, Spade) are made from Iron. Craft puts the ingot in the fire and the player strikes three times while its glow is in the band, which widens with Strength and Agility, three in the band being a clean craft one quality step better and Quick craft skipping the heat. Pieces are Crude, Standard, Fine, Superior or Masterwork, with a clean heat, specialisation and Warforged Steel ingot each adding a step. Crude wears out sooner, Fine lasts longer and is lighter, Superior and Masterwork also come enchanted (Magic and Rare), and a Masterwork carries its maker's name. A tool's quality is its life, 37 uses Crude up to 65. A craft gives 20 XP a tier of its metal plus 500 the first time that recipe is made, a quarter for recipes well below rank, and every piece shows its quality and maker with a provenance record kept by the server. At an anvil (Smithing) or a loom (Outfitting), a piece can be tempered one quality step better, Crude to Standard, Standard to Fine, Fine to Superior. To temper or reforge a worn piece, take it off first.
Standing ||| Standing is how regions and the law see the player. A negative standing now recovers faster, a point every 7 days instead of every 112, with the law or a guild. A screen shows every region that knows the player's name, how it sees them and what it will cost, every change from a crime, sentence, penance, contract or raid defence is announced in a line, and the Enhanced Plus Standing page has a Dress line showing what the outfit is doing. For a seat, a low Tithe (half the cap or less) gives +2 each week, a high one (over three quarters) gives minus 3, and a seat held without a challenge gives +5, with the seat's board saying which way the Standing moved since the last Turning.
Stores ||| The Stores hold what the player gathers, up to 5,000 of each material per character, and are kept in town, so the Stores page in any town moves materials. The page has filters, search and sorting, and Withdraw to pack takes up to 200 at a time, every material able to be withdrawn, with herbs coming out as Daggerfall's own northern or southern ingredient that works in the potion maker. Emptying the bag into the pack is possible from the Stores page anywhere, and a withdrawal whose answer was lost is sent again when opening the page, never minted twice. When the Stores have no room for what a craft would refine, the box says so.
Terrain ||| Terrain was moved off the main thread so the crossing hitch dies, the terrain block is built once a frame, and the terrain scale Daggerfall Unity streams at was adopted. Rolling hills run across the whole country, gentle in some regions and rolling hard in others and taller on the high ground, and rivers and streams cut their own valleys with the water at the bottom. Each climate has its own landform: mountains have long ridgelines that hold their line for kilometres with spurs and smooth valleys, the Alik'r has dune seas lying across the wind with a long rise to windward and a steep slip face to lee, swamps are near-flat with shallow hollows and low hummocks, and rainforests have karst towers standing over plains. Where climates meet, their lands blend across about a map pixel.
Textures ||| Textures had incident fixes, one shape for every texture door, and performance work that deleted work instead of reducing quality (PERF-TEX/TEX2/UI and MENU1-WARM). The steel plate cuirass has its skirt, painted with its own texture.
Titles ||| Titles are worn from the account card beside an aura or glyph, some given to particular players: SirMcMobdon, Tabby, Flylighter (Crystal Fist, with three purple slashes, alongside Disciple), Penitent (Diggleborf's, gold fading into sky blue), Aegis of Oblivion (Sureme's, violet, with a glyph of three pillars through a ring) and Primarch (made for GA00250, in light gold with a three-barred cross glyph). Players who first played on the evening of September 24 in the Americas hold the Founder title. Changing the aura, buying an insignia or unlinking Patreon no longer hides a Charter's title.
Town Layouts ||| Town layouts are the town mods' redrawn towns. Houses, rooms, quest buildings, smith items, Recall anchors and indoor saves keep their layout, and if a town cannot be shown in the house's layout the house is not treated as the player's there. Online, a home cannot be bought until the game has loaded the towns as other players see them, players who see a town differently are never put in the same building, both mods are set for the whole room and every home keeps its town's layout from purchase. Fixes covered building colours for climate, saves made inside new-town buildings, windmills on the new farms, the Order of the Raven's halls, the castles of Daggerfall, Sentinel and Wayrest, temple doors blocked by foundations, quest people appearing under floors or in attics, 34 Arkay temples left with no houses, and crop fields growing through walls. Town ponds no longer show flat painted squares, six lone island tiles are dry ground, and some pond edges the puddle removal had wrongly cleared are back. Every player in a room stands on the same ground, even if a town pack failed to load for one of them.
Travel ||| Travel on the map passes the days of the journey for the player's character online as it does offline. An enemy or duel opponent coming near closes a road hunt offer or ends the search, and a hunt's time passes when the result is closed. Wilderness enemies only come for a fast traveller who fails a Stealth check, with a noticing enemy wearing a !, fast travel remembers the speed chosen, long journeys are no longer refused when a route exists or interrupted by hunting prompts, and a landfall with no safe shore stops the journey and keeps the boat in the water. Online, the ship's time dial is gone and time at sea keeps the world's pace.
Weapons ||| Weapons move as before Diverse Weapons, and Daedric and other weapons show their own model so a longsword is no longer drawn as a katana. The weapon leaves the hand while a spell is readied.
Website ||| The website was cut to half the words, written plainer with one text face, without pictures, a horse disclaimer or Ko-fi in the credits, and later gained Ko-fi beside Patreon in its top-right corner. The Discord link is discord.gg/daggerfallonline and the download buttons always give the newest version for the player's system. The site now uses the game's stone and brass.
Welkynd Shards ||| Shards worn as a crystal are no longer spent by the Reforge or a shop. At the Mages Guild, scry names a kind of foe and the guild marks the nearest dungeon of theirs in the player's region that isn't on the map yet, for 3 Welkynd Shards and 500 gold.
Woods ||| Woods are the tree kinds by tier: Pine (tier 1), Oak (2), Cherry (3), Teak (4), Mahogany (5), and Ironwood and Ghostwood (6). Woodlands grow Oak and Cherry, mountains Pine and Oak, swamps Oak, subtropics Cherry and Teak, rainforests Teak and Mahogany, and deserts no trees. One tree in twenty is Ironwood in the rainforest or Ghostwood in the haunted woodlands, on vouched-for ground only, and ground nobody has vouched for grows nothing above tier 2. Mountain woods grow on foothills, half rolling and half ridges, and haunted woods on broken ground gashed by ravines and studded with crags.
