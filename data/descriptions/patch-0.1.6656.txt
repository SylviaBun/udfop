Grass ||| Grass is the ground cover. Its default style is Meadow: hand-drawn tall and short green tufts, flowering tufts, dry tufts and bushes on crossed cards like the low-poly trees, each growing to its own size, with flowers in lush patches and dry grass in poor ones. It takes its colour and exactly the same light as the ground under it in every climate and season, including a texture mod's ground, stands on slopes, sways in the wind with stiffer bushes, and far grass fades into the ground's colour. Grass is off by default; Settings > Grass turns it on at Full, Half or Quarter and picks the style, Meadow, Pixel (the old tufts) or Smooth.
Wind ||| Wind has wisps, a quiet wind loop, flora that sways, wind seen and heard off one mapping, and the sky's direction. With Enhanced sounds on, outdoor wind is a soft steady rush rising and falling, not a repeated moan. The Sway setting moves both grass and trees, and turning it off holds both still.
