Carpentry ||| Carpentry is a crafting profession that turns planks into bows, staves, arrows, furniture and the Foraging Basket. Crafting is done at a Furniture Store (50 gold a craft or saw) or a home Workbench station (50,000 gold), and each recipe needs the player's Carpentry rank for its wood. Bows come in every wood, which decides the bow's material from Pine as Iron up to Ghostwood as Ebony, and need a Resin. Craft starts a plane: drawing along the grain in 1.2 to 4 seconds gives a clean pass worth one quality step, and Quick craft skips it. A craft gives 20 XP a tier of its wood plus 500 the first time a recipe is made.

Climbing ||| Climbing lets the player pull up onto ledges, wall tops, parapets and roofs by pressing Jump at them, and hang from edges. Climbing skill sets reach, speed, grip time and how long a fall can still be caught, with every point past 100 making the player faster up to about 1.4 times at 200. From the ground a shoulder-height ledge is reachable at skill 0 and one above the head at 100, a full pack lowers reach, and a roof can be climbed from its eave up to 50 degrees. With Roleplay and Realism on, the player cannot climb while holding a weapon, and online others see the player on the wall and hear them climb.
