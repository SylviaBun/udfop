Account Card ||| The account card shows a player's registered date and time played, and a row of Renown for each of their characters. The level beside the player's name is that of the last character played.
Account Recovery ||| Account recovery lets an operator issue a recovery code (RECOVER-OP) so a player can regain access to an account.
Accounts ||| Accounts hold a player's login and their cloud saves, served by an account Worker that issues tokens and checks passwords. The service was built in 0.1.3607 and 0.1.3690, then repaired after an outage caused by an overly costly password-hashing setting on Cloudflare, and later given a clock fix.
Acrobat ||| Acrobat is a character skill. Its only recorded change is a fix in patch 0.1.4320.
Aim Arcs ||| Aim arcs are the lines drawn for ranged and naval aiming. They are clean lines that read the same at any range, with a dash along each flight.
Aim Line ||| The aim line is the readout under the crosshair. It sits together with the volley count and wraps, and on a small screen it shows only the range and whether the player is on target.
Aiming ||| Aiming covers how naval guns are aimed at ships. With the crosshair on an enemy hull the guns aim at that spot, and chain shot goes for the sails while cannon still hit the hull. The aim turns red only when the guns will really hit, goes grey when they cannot fire, and says why, whether reloading, braced, out of fire barrels or guns silent.
Alchemy ||| Alchemy is a crafting profession for brewing potions. The cheapest potions are available from rank 0, resistances and Restore Power from rank 10, Invisibility at rank 70 and Purification at rank 90. Every brew earns XP by the potion's rank, plus 500 the first time each potion is brewed.
Alchemy Station ||| An alchemy station brews Daggerfall's twenty potions, at any Alchemist's for 50 gold a brew or at a home station. Ingredients come from the player's Stores. A brew makes one potion, two from Journeyman and three as a Master, and may come out Potent with +25% magnitude for healing, stamina, strength or power potions. Daggerfall's own potion maker is unchanged and earns nothing toward Alchemy.
Ally Cast ||| Ally Cast is an engine feature, ALLY-CAST, added in 0.1.3773 together with its audit.
Ambient Text ||| Ambient Text is a mod by Regnier, version 1.8, ported one to one into the game and audited.
Angler ||| Angler is a fishing rank. At rank 50 it gives a longer tug window.
AoE Capsule Sweep ||| The AoE capsule sweep is an engine fix to how area-of-effect checks sweep through a capsule shape.
Apostle ||| Apostle is an online feature added in 0.1.4166.
Apothecary ||| The Apothecary is a guild seat building. A seat's holder can build it, and tier 1 needs 100 Cut Stone, 100 Oak Planks and 1,000 silver. In its own town it makes jewellery one quality step better, gives half again the Cooking XP for dishes (two and a half times with a clean pan at tier 2) and adds a +10% chance of Potent brews. Only the holding guild's members benefit, and only in that town.
Arcane Essence ||| Arcane Essence is a crafting material. It stays in the player's Stores, never the pack, and sells on the market like any Stores material.
Archers ||| Archers is an engine fix so that archers aim at the target they selected.
Area Spells ||| Area spells hit everything inside an area. Buff-only Area spells also land on the caster. Area at Range and Around Caster now show what they catch, with people up to 4.45 m and the boss at 5.8 m. The shockwave spreads across what the spell hit, bigger areas show more, and area heals fill the circle.
Arena Bouts ||| An arena bout is a fight on the sand in which nobody dies. At 1 health the fighter goes down, and at 15% or less they can sheathe to yield. The healers make everyone whole afterwards, purses run from 50 gold for a first-tier win to 10,000 for the Grand Champion, and progress, titles and record are saved with the character. The player cannot rest, travel or leave through the gates during a bout, and set bonuses, sigil weapon bonuses and Legendary powers switch off against other players.
Arena Crowd ||| The arena crowd is made of Daggerfall's own people, and big bouts draw hundreds. It cheers great blows, gasps at critical strikes, groans at knockdowns and boos stalling, and each fighter has its favour, with a darling and a villain marked on screen. At the verdict it throws flowers for a loved winner. Its voices come from Daggerfall's own crowd recordings, and it no longer seats nude figures while Show Nudity is off.
Arena Gate ||| The arena's north gate is where the Herald, gate wardens, the Red and Blue Banner recruiters and a bookmaker stand. The Herald offers to watch the exhibition, fight on the ladder, open the Arena window, visit the fighters' hall or leave, in short plain choices.
Arena Ladder ||| The arena ladder has ten tiers, from The Pit to The Grand Melee. Winning three bouts leads to the Tier Champion, and every tier's champion is an elite. Opponents do not grow with the player. Losing a ladder bout resets that tier's run, potions and cheese spells are barred, and repeat wins pay no purse, points or Renown. The online ladder needs a registered account, and each ticket is good for one bout.
Arena Online ||| Online arena bouts match real players. A bout is fought on its own copy of the arena floor, refereed by the server, and needs a registered account, while guests can only watch. Players are matched at a season rating that starts at 1,000, or choose a Casual bout where nothing is counted. Leaving a fight for more than 15 seconds forfeits it. Anyone can watch from the Bouts page, up to 60 watchers, and realm leaderboards show climbs, ratings and banners.
Arena Stands ||| The arena stands are where spectators sit. Watchers can no longer jump down onto the sand during bouts, though a private session's host still can. Players in the stands are visible to each other and to the fighters, and private sessions show only their members.
Arena Titles ||| Arena titles are earned by climbing the ladder. The Herald calls the player by their title on the sand, it shows beside the arena banner on the character sheet and on letters and notices, and Grand Champion is awarded once the realm counts forty wins.
Arena Undercroft ||| The undercroft is a stair inside the colosseum leading down to Kamer's 32-block dungeon, now the fighters' hall. Nothing there attacks the player or breaks their rest. The Pit Master offers practice bouts against a fighter of the player's own tier and the Keeper of the Hall reads the Hall of Champions. Its deep cellars past the hall have enemies again, and the Smaller Dungeons setting never shrinks it.
Arena Window ||| The Arena window is a single window for everything at the arena, opened by the Herald, the recruiters, the bookmaker or the Arena button. Its pages are Bouts, Ladder, Team, Leaderboards, Records and Rules, with short buttons such as Bet, Find match, Host, Join, Cancel bout and Replay.
Arena of Daggerfall ||| The Arena of Daggerfall is a colosseum in the heart of Daggerfall city, Kamer's Daggerfall Arena, used with his permission. It stands in every game with either city layout, is drawn with the player's own copy of Daggerfall's textures and dungeon pieces, and has walkable tiers and a floor. It replaced the tavern, two gem stores and houses on its block, whose occupants, quests, rooms and homes were moved or redirected elsewhere in the city.
Argonian ||| Argonian is a rendering topic. Its only recorded change fixes an Argonian shown on a human Morrowind body.
Armour Display ||| Armour display shows each body part's armour on its own panel, plus one Armour figure at the figure's head.
Arms ||| Arms covers rendering faults in first-person and body arms. Black arms, three arms and arm jitter were each fixed.
Arrest ||| Arrest is a world topic with a single recorded fix in 0.1.4582.
Arrow Origins ||| Arrow origins is an engine fix so that arrows start from the right place.
Arrows ||| Arrows are crafted ammunition. A second recipe uses 1 Pine Plank, 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Harpy Feathers to make twenty arrows. A double-arrow fault was fixed.
Ascension Window ||| The Ascension window is the character screen for ascending. It was added and audited, opens whenever the player asks, and every route to the sheet now checks the door, not just the key.
Attic Glare ||| Attic glare is a rendering fault that was fixed.
Attribute Box ||| The attribute box is part of the character sheet. A fix stopped it showing raw macros.
Auctions ||| Auctions let a player sell a Masterwork piece to the highest bidder. The opening bid is 1 to 1,000,000 Marks with a fee of 1% of it, and an auction stands 24 hours in its region and counts among the seller's 30 listings. Each bid must beat the standing one by 5% and at least 1 Mark, and a bid in the last 2 minutes adds 2 minutes. The winning bid pays the seller less a 5% sales tax, and the piece goes to the winner at once at a board in the region, otherwise after the courier's time. Bids are held from the bidder's Marks and returned if outbid, and a piece with no bids comes back.
Audit 62 ||| Audit 62 was an audit pass covering the changes since AUDIT 58. It fixed 39 findings in six reviewed lanes, integrated onto main, plus three leftover items.
Audit 63 ||| Audit 63 covered the systems that the fault-shape audits had under-sampled. It fixed 50 findings in seven reviewed lanes.
Audit 64 ||| Audit 64 was a second round of subsystem-depth audit, covering the ten classic systems AUDIT 63 did not reach.
Audit 65 ||| Audit 65 was a two-round audit. A stub was published to claim the number, round one fixed findings in eight lanes and was recorded, and round two reviewed and merged six lanes before its record closed.
Audit 68 ||| Audit 68 was a whole-tree sweep, recorded as partial, in 0.1.4023.
Audit Air 1 ||| Audit Air 1 was a fix that came from a field report and its audit.
Audit Death 1 ||| Audit Death 1 was a fix that came from a field report and its audit.
Auras ||| Auras are cosmetic effects worn from the aura picker on the account card, each granted with a title. They include the Oblivion Ward, Golden Radiance, the Holo Shadow Cloak (with the Shadow Fang title), the developers' Seraph Wings and Crystal Resonance (with the Crystal Fist title). An aura now shows to everyone nearby within a second or two, up to 32 are drawn at once, and the cloak and wings fold away while riding or swimming. They sit correctly on both Morrowind bodies and Eye of the Beholder sprites.
Auto-Travel ||| Auto-travel no longer crashes when it stops beside a spawned dungeon.
Backups ||| Backups received newer backup support in 0.1.4454.
Bandages ||| Bandages is an items topic. Its only recorded change is a fix in 0.1.3895.
Banishment ||| Banishment is a punishment only murder and treason can bring, and it lifts after 30 days. A pardon can be bought at a temple in that region, where the watch does not enter. Online, banishment follows the realm's calendar so a wrong computer clock cannot end it early.
Bank ||| The Bank holds gold and makes loans. Online, one account is kept for the player at every bank, drawable at any other, and houses, ships, guild deposits, the market, homes and rent use that one account. A loan still belongs to the region where it was taken and the account repays it. Every online loan was forgiven in a one-time amnesty, the bank window names the region whose account it shows, and the Bank's card shows combat silver earned today.
Bank Ships ||| Bank ships link a ship's cabin to the bank. With several same-size ships the game asks which gets the cabin, and when the linked ship is not afloat the message tells the player to summon her from Holdings, Fleet.
Banners ||| Banners are two things: capture points and arena companies. A banner point is raised by standing with no living enemy within 8 m for 20 seconds, and a contested banner freezes. The arena's Red Banner and Blue Banner companies take fighters at the gate for free, score a point for each ladder win (3 for a Tier Champion, 10 for the Grand Champion), and the banner with more points at season's end wins and its fighters wear the laurel next year.
Barracks ||| A seat's Barracks sends 2, 4 or 6 town guards to fight for the holder, each at a post.
Base64 Payload ||| Base64 payload is an engine performance fix that handles a megabyte of base64 output efficiently.
Basic Roads ||| Basic Roads is an engine topic about road generation. It had no switch, which was fixed, and its work was merged, audited and gated.
Basket ||| The basket is the herb-gathering search. A glint appears near the crosshair with a ring showing how long it has left, and finds show as diamonds. The mouse cursor appears for the search so glints can be clicked, and clicking anywhere while a glint shows still counts. With Gentle acts on there is nothing to press.
Battle ||| Battles at a seat can be left with Leave, Close, /leave, walking out or dying. Each battle is announced in red at the Turning, a day, an hour and five minutes before.
Battlefield ||| A siege room lets in only signed fighters and up to 60 spectators. After a fall a fighter rises at their side's camp every 20 seconds, or 30 at a crown.
Beautiful Cities ||| Beautiful Cities is a mod pair, both on by default, each with a switch under Mods that applies on the next start. City walls no longer have 3-metre gaps beside their corner towers.
Beautiful Cities of Daggerfall ||| Beautiful Cities of Daggerfall is a mod by carademono that lays out all 410 cities again with districts, walls, gates, markets and docks.
Beautiful Villages ||| Beautiful Villages fixes floating scenery. Trees now stand on the ground drawn beneath them, and houses and the stone gazebo no longer float because hills are drawn at their real size and shape.
Beautiful Villages of Daggerfall ||| Beautiful Villages of Daggerfall is a mod by carademono that lays out every village, hamlet, farm, manor and temple again. Roadside taverns keep their inn, and 274 with no tavern block stay as in Daggerfall.
Bedroll ||| The bedroll is a camping item with 10 nights. It gives a rough rest point wherever a camp could be placed, dungeons included, and rest starts after ten seconds.
Better Ambience ||| Better Ambience is a mod by Joshua Steinhauer, version 0.1.4, ported one to one from the sources the bundle carries. It is audited and at peace with Immersive Footsteps.
Billboards ||| Billboards is a rendering topic. They are sorted by texture for speed, and the billboard key follows the record.
Black Screen ||| Black screen was an interface fault fixed by resolving a namespace over one end of a module cycle.
Blade Contact ||| Blade contact finds the piece under a blade by arithmetic, a performance improvement.
Bleeding ||| Bleeding is the port's own bleeding effect.
Blood ||| Blood is the port's own blood effect. Droplets fly from the wound under gravity and leave their mark on floor, wall or ceiling, with ceiling drops dripping back down. Spatter lands the way the blow threw it in one to three uneven clumps, pools are several blobs with fingers where the blood ran, and stain art is 128 pixels per stain, generated from noise and built in the background so the first hit does not stutter.
Boarding ||| Boarding is how a ship is captured. After battering a ship until she strikes her colours, the player comes alongside, slows right down and presses Activate to throw grapples, which puts out her fires. Her crew and captain fight on her deck, and killing the captain and most of the crew takes her. The player's own crew comes along, and the player lands across from where they stood. Pirates can board the player too, with only one pirate raid at a time, and they fall back and cast off if beaten or timed out, then wait five minutes before grappling again.
Boat Deeds ||| A boat deed works in every port town and looks for a port all around the player. If no port is near it names the nearest one and which way it lies.
Boat Menu ||| The boat menu appears under the crosshair when the player looks at any part of their boat, like the loot list. Options include Take the helm, Board, Open storage, Pick up, Change style, Status and Position. Looking at the helm, chest or ladder lights that option first, and an option that cannot be used shows its reason. Ships from a deed cannot be picked up, and on a phone, the classic interface or indoors a tap or click on the hull gives the list.
Boats ||| Boats can be placed and kept. Crossing water works with Iliac Puddle No More on, and a boat placed underground is kept with its hold when the player leaves unless that setting is turned back off in Come Sail Away's settings. Boats lost under the ground or on the seabed are returned as parts, a crewed ship is left where it is, and a boat whose hold is too heavy can no longer be picked up into unmoveable parts.
Bodies ||| Bodies rest on slopes after a fix in 0.1.3942.
Body ||| Body is a rendering topic with a single fix, for a hole in the body.
Body Interaction ||| Body interaction lets the player press F on a body.
Bookmaker ||| The bookmaker at the arena takes wagers of 10 to 1,000 gold on the hour's exhibition, one a bout, until it begins. Odds come from the fighters' records less the house's tenth, a win pays the stake plus the odds, and no price is shorter than 1 to 5. The wager settles even if the player leaves before the fight, though leaving once it has begun forfeits the stake. Winnings are collected in person at his stall.
Books ||| Books are a platform feature. A Daggerfall book is read in project-raum's animated book, books appear in the test packs, and a book that never showed was fixed.
Boulders ||| Boulders can be quarried for stone. They glow and show on the compass, and the player presses E at "Quarry the stone". Each square holds a number of boulders a day: 1 to 3 in woodland, haunted woodland and subtropical squares, 2 to 4 in mountain woods, and 3 to 5 in mountains and deserts. A rock field's boulders now all appear.
Bounties ||| Bounties are hunting notices taken from boards. A player can hold up to 4, each lasting 24 in-game hours (about two real hours) and shown on the Overworld as a black ring that can be clicked to walk to the hunt. Every player sees the same notices, while monster tier and reward come from the player's own level. A party can share a bounty with members in the same tier, who share one pack, kill count and pay at their own levels. An abandoned bounty keeps its kills, and online a bounty stays yours until finished or dropped.
Bounty Boards ||| Bounty boards are notice boards in towns with two or more notice boards, where half become Bounty Boards. Every board in a town posts the same four hunts to every player, and notices change each in-game day. Pressing one opens the board window in the Enhanced Plus style.
Bounty Contracts ||| Finishing a bounty contract improves the player's standing with that region's law.
Bounty Hunts ||| A bounty hunt is the fight a notice sends the player to. An open-ground notice marks a land spot 4 to 10 map pixels from the town with a black circle, and walking onto it summons 4 to 8 monsters whose kinds depend on the player's level, from Rats and Giant Bats up to Gargoyles, Wraiths and Daedroths. A real dungeon 4 to 10 pixels away can instead be entered, and a farm notice builds a farmstead copied from a nearby farm. A pack of 6 to 8 is hunted in two groups, and if the pack despawns the kills are kept and only survivors return.
Bounty Rewards ||| Killing the last monster completes the bounty, and a reward notice pauses the game, tells a short story and lists exactly what was received. A full pack of 8 pays the tier's full purse and each monster fewer takes 10% off, from 30 to 50 gold at tier 1 up to about 150 to 200 at level 15 and above. The item reward is one random weapon or armour piece, never higher than blue, and its condition and chance of being Magic rise with the tier.
Bow ||| Bow is an engine topic. The bow salvage was done and the bow scale was fixed.
Bowyer ||| The Bowyer comes with Carpentry 50 and makes bows one quality step better.
Brace Button ||| The brace button stays full finger size on a phone at any HUD scale, and its warning says "Broadside - Brace".
Bracing ||| Bracing no longer leaves the player crouched after letting go of the key, which also knocked aim short.
Brewer ||| Brewer is a rank-50 reward that gives three potions a brew at Journeyman.
Bring Online ||| Bringing a character online starts it in the world's present and it is never charged for time away. It switches the online copy to Oblivion Remastered leveling, carrying over level, skills and earned level-ups, with the skill bar starting empty. A Never saved character's tile says Undo bringing in, which gives back everything that came with it, and a message says when a character was played on another of the player's accounts.
Broadside Camera ||| The broadside camera moves the view out over the side being aimed to show ports, splash and the enemy, and returns when released. It can be turned off in Features, Naval Combat, Broadside camera.
Broker Wares ||| Broker wares were reworked so that they are bound and dismantled.
Builder ||| Builder is a rank-50 reward that makes a seat's fortification projects the player begins need 10% less stone.
Burning Court ||| The Burning Court is a world feature added in 0.1.4255.
Butcher ||| Butcher is a rank-100 reward that gives two Raw Meat a body instead of one, and the meat withdrawn spoils half as fast.
Buy Orders ||| Buy orders are standing orders to buy a material, up to 5,000 units at the player's price and 10,000,000 Marks in all. The Marks are held when the order is posted. Anyone at a board in that region can fill it from their Stores, in whole or in part, and is paid the price less 5%, with the units going into the player's Stores at once. An order stands 7 days, the unfilled part comes back when withdrawn, a player can have 20 up at once, and materials nothing yields yet cannot be ordered.
Calmer Eye ||| Calmer Eye is its own switch, off by default, in Features, Enhanced lighting.
Camera Kick ||| Camera kick makes each gun kick the camera as it fires, so a broadside rolls down the ship. A powder barrel going off on the player's deck shakes harder than a cannonball.
Campfire ||| The Campfire is a camping item with eight nights of fuel. Placing it is free and sleeping at one uses one night, and when it burns down it goes cold and stays cold until relit with fuel or picked up. Looking at a fire or tent offers Rest here, Cook food, Relight and Pick up. It costs 40 gold, half that online, and General Stores and Pawn Shops never sell out of them. One left in a dungeon comes out with the player, and campfires and tents on a boat's deck stay on the boat as she sails.
Campfire Kit ||| General Stores and Pawn Shops always stock a Campfire Kit and 99 Rations, and online they never run out. With Climates and Calories off the kit can be placed, rested at, cooked at, stoked and packed.
Camping ||| Camping is a survival topic with a single change, CAMP-NOTIMER.
Camps ||| Camps stand 100 to 150 m out. Placing a tent or Campfire with four already out now packs away the player's oldest camp instead of refusing.
Card ||| Card is an interface topic with a single fix, for a card that folds.
Carpenter's Stores ||| Carpenter's Stores cost 314 gold, down from 336, and each repairs more. A Carrack now takes eighteen.
