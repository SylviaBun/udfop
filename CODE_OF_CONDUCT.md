# Code of Conduct

UDFOP is a fan-made reference for Daggerfall Online, written and corrected by its community. This applies to everything connected to the project: the website, the Game Guide pages, issues, pull requests and any other discussion.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful.** Disagree about facts, not people. Criticise a page or an edit, never the person who wrote it.
- **Be welcoming.** New players and first-time contributors are the point of this project. Be patient with questions and mistakes.
- **Be honest and helpful.** Say how you know something, correct errors openly, and accept corrections graciously.
- **Keep it on topic.** The project is about Daggerfall Online information. Do not use it to advertise, spam or campaign.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hateful content of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as real names, addresses or accounts, without their consent.
- Deliberately false or misleading information, vandalism, or attempts to break or exploit the site.
- Copying other people's work, or the developers' release text, into the project.

## Moderation

The maintainers may edit, hide or remove any page, comment, issue or pull request that breaks this code, ask people to change their behaviour, and block people who do not. Decisions are made in good faith and will be explained where it is reasonable to do so. The maintainers are volunteers, and no response time is promised.

## Reporting a problem

- For something on GitHub, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request, or mention it to the maintainer, [tau-samsara](https://github.com/tau-samsara).
- For something on a page, use [Feedback and bugs](https://tau-samsara.github.io/udfop/#/feedback).

Please do not include private details of other people in a public report.
